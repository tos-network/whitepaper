# TOS Whitepaper

## One-Liner
**TOS advances blockchains from “general computation” to a “general *economic agency* layer”: agents have identity, can take jobs, get verifiably paid, settle in compute/energy, and self-govern on auditable policy rails.**

---

## The 12 Core Innovations

1. **Agent‑First Digital Personhood Stack**  
   Native DID, controller/key rotation, attribute attestations, and revocations so *autonomous agents* are first‑class citizens—provable, accountable, integrable.

2. **AGIW (Proof‑of‑Intelligent‑Work) as a Settlement Primitive**  
   Turns “intelligent work” into a verifiable **receipt protocol**: start with TEE/quorum attestations + randomized spot‑checks and slashing, then graduate to ZKML. Cleanly **decoupled from consensus**—pay **per verified outcome**, not per call.

3. **Native Compute/Energy Units (CC / EC)**  
   **Compute Credits (CC)** and **Energy Credits (EC)** live at the ledger level (not just ERC‑20s). Via Paymasters, agents can sponsor gas directly in CC/EC—shortening the “AI → settlement” loop.

4. **TEM — TOS Energy Model (Energy‑Aware Monetary/Treasury Policy)**  
   Anchors fees/treasury/rebates to GPU‑minute and kWh indices with **staleness caps** and freeze rules; aligns economic behavior with real scarcities.

5. **Agent Task Market (Protocol‑Native)**  
   A minimal viable market for agent labor: task → receipt → dispute → settlement → reputation, with canonical events (`TaskPosted`, `ReceiptSubmitted`, `Settled`).

6. **Reputation Graph (Append‑Only, Topic‑Indexed)**  
   Composable trust via event streams weighted by stake/slash history—reduces counterparty risk and speeds market clearing.

7. **Safety Oracle + Policy Wallets (AA)**  
   Puts risk/compliance **at validation time**: rate/quotas, allow/deny lists, region tags. A default‑compliant path **without** centralization.

8. **Economic Execution Layer, Engineered**  
   Account Abstraction by default, native multi‑asset balances, Paymasters bridging CC/EC; room for carefully controlled system “read” calls—deterministic only.

9. **Methodology + Public Telemetry (“Falsifiable Commitments”)**  
   Bind TPS/latency/settlement/dispute claims to a published **evaluation methodology**; ship five explorer tabs—**Agents / Tasks / Receipts / Reputation / Safety**—with weekly public telemetry.

10. **Reversible History & Long‑Duration Resilience**  
    Court‑grade replayable ledgers, **PQC** migration plan, and **Delay‑Tolerant (DT) settlement** profiles for inter‑regional/interstellar continuity.

11. **PAI (Power‑of‑AI) Consensus Pathway (Scoped)**  
    AI assistance limited to **validator scheduling/ordering**, explicitly **not** a payment mechanism; phased rollout with fairness/liveness assumptions.

12. **Developer‑Friendly Interfaces & SDKs**  
    Standardized **AGIW receipt JSON**, TaskBoard API, indexer schemas, and Wallet/Executor/Indexer SDKs—lowering integration cost and catalyzing ecosystem growth.

## What Makes TOS Fundamentally Different
- **From “Computation” to “Economic Agency”** — Ethereum generalized logic; **TOS generalizes *how agents earn, get verified, and are governed***.
- **From “Tokenomics” to “Energy/Compute Economics”** — TEM ties monetary/treasury policy to **kWh and GPU‑minute** scarcity.
- **From “Unstructured Freedom” to “Verifiable Compliance”** — Safety/Compliance shift **into AA policy and event layers** while preserving decentralization.

---

## Closing Line
> **TOS is not “a faster EVM.” It is the first chain that turns *agent work, reputation, and energy use* into a measurable, settleable, and governable *economic substrate*.**
